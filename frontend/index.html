HOW IT WORKS

Deterministic by design.

Four cohesive stages ensure that thread starvation is diagnosed and resolved automatically, with zero manual priority tuning required.

01 Detection

Bypass Tracking

When a newly arriving or active higher-priority process is dispatched, all eligible processes currently waiting in the ready queue have their individual bypass counter (Bp) incremented.

scheduler/preemptive.py
02 Evaluation

Starvation Scoring

At each scheduling quantum, waiting time and bypass counts are evaluated against the starvation formula. If Sp reaches or exceeds θ = 20, the thread is formally flagged as starving.

starvation/detector.py
03 Prevention

Adaptive Aging

The aging engine promotes the starving process by reducing its numerical priority value (e.g. from priority 6 down to priority 3), immediately qualifying it for CPU allocation under preemptive arbitration.

aging/engine.py
04 Equilibrium

Guaranteed Rollback

Once an aged process finishes its execution slice or completes, its original base priority is seamlessly restored. High-priority threads never suffer from permanent priority inversion.

aging/aging_manager.py
MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION

The Starvation Metric Equation

Sp = Wp + (Bp × α)
Sp Composite Starvation Score for Process p
Wp Accumulated ready-queue waiting duration (clock ticks)
Bp Number of times a higher-priority process bypassed p
α Bypass weight coefficient (configured = 2.0)
θ Intervention threshold (configured = 20.0 score units)
